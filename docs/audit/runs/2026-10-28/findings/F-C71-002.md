# F-C71-002 — “never editable” ARES fields have ambiguous enforcement

- Source: `6e75a173e822e4c4be42821b0a4af48ca457bd36`.
- Severity: Low; policy/implementation divergence, no escalation established.
- Evidence: locally reproduced; reviewed and amended to preserve the policy ambiguity.
- Known status: new observation. Disposition: audit only; a later policy decision is needed.

SPEC §5 calls `META.outputDir`, `LESSONS[].substrand` and `LESSONS[].aresKeywords` “never editable by anyone” and cites the whitelist as its authority. However, `storedNotEdited` explicitly promises presentation-only hiding; the whitelist protects teachers, while the Subject Administrator branch restores only META identity keys and otherwise passes content through.

An authentic Subject Administrator's save-as-new request changed all three fields and returned200. Trusted rereads confirmed those values in the new candidate and an unchanged source. `app/tests/audit/field-omission.ts` records the reproduction. The three fields are not the separately enforced resource links or lesson numbers.

Sonnet's original-author response agrees with the observed behaviour but objects to calling it a vulnerability without resolving the wording. Sol accepts that amendment: record a specification/enforcement ambiguity, not a confirmed escalation. No automatic change to Combine behaviour or harmful downstream result is claimed. The July workaround avoiding field-level access remains intentional and is not itself a finding. A later change must decide whether administrators are allowed to alter these hidden fields, then align SPEC and enforcement.
