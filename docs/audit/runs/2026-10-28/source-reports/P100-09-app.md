## Candidates

**1. Mobile jump nav hidden while editing can remain live (lead, low severity, confirmed only as a comment conflict).**
- Evidence: `custom.scss:2748-2751` says that at ≤640px "editing is unavailable," so the nav has no purpose and is hidden. `custom.scss:2837-2841` says the edit-unlock guard runs on mount, so "a session unlocked at a wider width SURVIVES a resize."
- Preconditions: unlock editing at a wide viewport, then resize to ≤640px.
- Expected, per 2748: editing is unavailable at this width. Actual, per 2838: the session stays unlocked, and the rule at 2751 removes the nav while editing continues.
- The CSS does not itself break editing. The user-visible effect is that an active editor loses in-form navigation, and the stated rationale is false for that state.
- Focused test: Playwright at 1024px, unlock, resize to 375px. Assert whether the editing controls remain active and whether `.lesson-controls__nav` is hidden.

**2. `.lesson-controls__ext` may hide more than the decorative glyph (lead, medium severity, unverified).**
- Evidence: `custom.scss:2769-2771` sets `display: none` on the whole `.lesson-controls__ext` class. The comment at 2758-2768 says it hides only the ↗ glyph, and that both buttons keep `aria-label`s so assistive technology still announces them.
- Precondition: `.lesson-controls__ext` is a wrapper that contains the "Quick preview" or "Formatted PDF" buttons, not just a glyph element.
- Expected: both preview buttons stay visible and usable at 375px, as the comment describes. Actual: unknown until the markup is checked; if it is a wrapper, both buttons disappear on phones.
- Focused test: Playwright at 375px, assert both preview buttons are visible and enabled in the version editor's control bar.
