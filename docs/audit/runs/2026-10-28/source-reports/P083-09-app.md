**C3 — Make Official asks about a previous Official that may not exist (confirmed local; Low).**

If the user answers OK, the request sends `deletePrevious=true&expectedPreviousOfficialId=` (`:81-85`) with an empty precondition. How the server treats that is unknown.

- *Repro:* render with `officialVersionId={null}`, `canMakeOfficial={true}`; stub `window.confirm` and `fetch`; click Make Official; assert the confirm text and the URL.
