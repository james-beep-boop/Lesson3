### Candidates

**1. Summary-lesson title is capturable and restorable, but policy grants it to Subject Administrators only.** Severity: medium. Confidence: uncertain lead.
- Evidence: `projection.ts:205-206` and `:446` use `SUMMARY_LESSON_PROSE`. The policy excerpt says `SUMMARY_TABLE.lessons[].title` is Subject-Admin-only and is listed in `SUMMARY_LESSON_PROSE`. The header comment (`projection.ts:10-13`) says capture and teacher-write sets are kept in step by importing the same constants.
- Precondition: the policy excerpt is accurate and `SUMMARY_LESSON_PROSE` contains `title`. I could not read that constant.
- Expected: a teacher's capture should not hold, and a restore should not overlay, a title the teacher cannot write.
- Actual (inferred from policy, not read): `applyCapture` is role-blind and overlays `title` whenever the capture holds it.
- Focused test: build a source with `summaryTable.lessons[0] = {id:'s1', title:'A', observed:'x'}`, edit the title to `'B'`, and assert `projectCapture` does not contain `summaryLesson:s1.title`. Whether a teacher's restore persists the title depends on `applyEditorFieldSplit`, which is out of range.

**2. `captureFieldPath` accepts a keyed singleton and does not check the group exists.** Severity: low. Confidence: uncertain lead.
- Evidence: `projection.ts:458-470`. The singleton key has no row id (`:25-31`). Yet `captureFieldPath({}, 'finalExplanation:x', 'instructions')` returns `'finalExplanation.instructions'`, and a document without a `finalExplanation` group also gets a path.
- Expected: `null` for a keyed singleton and for a missing group. The doc comment says the leaf is "validated", so the caller may already filter these, which I cannot see.
- Test: assert `captureFieldPath({}, 'finalExplanation:x', 'instructions') === null`.

**3. Invalid `sourceUpdatedAt` throws instead of reporting.** Severity: low. Confidence: confirmed local reasoning; reachability uncertain.
- Evidence: `provenance.ts:23-26`. `new Date(String(null))` and `new Date(String(undefined))` produce an Invalid Date, and `toISOString()` throws `RangeError`.
- Precondition: a caller passes nullish or non-date `updatedAt`. `unsavedWork.ts:63-68` selects `updatedAt`, so this is not reachable from that call site under normal Payload behaviour.
- Test: `captureProvenance({baseUpdatedAt:'x', schemaVersion:'v1'}, undefined)` throws.

**4. Site Administrator visibility in Manage may be inconsistent.** Severity: unknown. Confidence: question only.
- Evidence: `unsavedWork.ts:49` admits Site Administrators through the early guard. `:73` then requires `isEditorFor(user, …)` per row. The policy says a Site Administrator has global access. Whether `isEditorFor` returns true for them is in `access`, which I did not read.
