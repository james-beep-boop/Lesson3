| Coordinator | Assignments, source review, handoffs, progress and synthesis | One writer for global records; fixed source baseline |
| Heavy-worker / primary test-target | Builds, scans, all suite types, fidelity, isolated full-stack attack tests, synthetic upgrade/restore drills | Own audit checkout per executor; pinned Node container; target identity/reset/mail proof; caps based on available resources and competing workloads |

ownership is reconciled; prefer a new run-specific project, volumes, directories and image tags.
All live work against the same audit target remains serialized. Independent workers may use distinct
targets only after C01 proof for each; they do not share mutable fixtures. Record host/load context for
performance measurements; worker capacity cannot establish deployment-host capacity.
